Santali Speaks

What it does

Santali Speaks reads Santali text written in Ol Chiki aloud. Copy Ol Chiki text in any app, open Santali Speaks, and tap Speak. It works offline for text you have already listened to.

Current status

Santali speech is not yet available. The speech service that would produce Ol Chiki audio needs an access key that this project does not have yet. Until that is in place, the app will tell you that speech is unavailable. It will never read your text with another language's voice.

Try the Ol Chiki text box

This box checks what you type or paste. It does not send anything anywhere.

Privacy

Your text stays on your phone. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and it does not track you.

About the font

Ol Chiki text is shown in Noto Sans Ol Chiki, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see licence).
